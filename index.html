Guess It
Maths

A Guess Who-style game for the maths classroom. Put a board on the screen, let one student pick a secret card, and the class asks questions until only one card is left.

How to play

  1. Pick a secret card

    Invite a student to the board to choose a card and show it only to you, or let the game pick one at random.

  2. Ask yes/no questions

    Students write a question on mini whiteboards. Choose one, answer it, and tap every card it rules out.

  3. Guess the card

    When one card is left, reveal the secret. Fewer questions means a better round.

About us

Guess It Maths is made by two maths graduates training to become teachers.

S. Tomlin

Studied Mathematics (BSc) at King’s College London and is training to teach secondary and sixth form maths, alongside his street photography.

E. Pettigrew

Graduated in Mathematics (BSc) from Swansea University and is training to teach maths at Key Stages 3, 4 and 5.

Where the idea came from

The game takes its format from the classic board game Guess Who, and we owe the idea to it. Our aim is to make that format genuinely useful in maths lessons: to win, students have to use precise vocabulary and think carefully about the properties of numbers, shapes and graphs.
